# PGDM Student Handbook — Student Support Policies (Academic Year 2026–27)

This handbook is the single policy source used by the Student Support Agent. Each numbered sub-section is one retrievable chunk.

## 1. General

### 1.1 About this handbook
This handbook covers fees, payments, scholarships, course and specialisation changes, attendance, examinations, ID cards and the student support desk for the PGDM programme (Big Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science). Where this handbook is silent, students should raise a support ticket with the Student Affairs office.

### 1.2 Student support desk and tickets
Students can raise a support ticket through the student support agent or the student portal. Every ticket receives a ticket ID. Standard tickets are answered within 2 working days; tickets marked high priority (payment failures, examination issues) are answered within 1 working day. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding institute holidays.

## 2. Fees

### 2.1 Fee structure and instalments
Programme fees are paid term-wise. Each term has a fee due date published on the student portal. Students may pay a term fee in full or in a maximum of two instalments; the second instalment must be paid within 30 days of the term due date.

### 2.2 Late payment fee
If the term fee is not paid in full by the due date, a late fee of INR 200 per day is charged, up to a maximum of INR 5,000 per term. After 30 days of non-payment, access to the learning management system (LMS) may be suspended until dues are cleared.

### 2.3 Payment portal failure
If the online payment portal fails or a transaction is declined on the due date because of a technical issue, the student may pay on the next working day without any late fee, provided that the student (a) raises a support ticket with category "fees" on the same day and (b) keeps a screenshot or transaction reference of the failed attempt. If money was debited but the payment does not show on the portal, the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5–7 working days; the student should not pay twice before the reversal is confirmed.

### 2.4 Fee refunds
Fees are refundable as follows: 100% (minus INR 1,000 processing charge) if withdrawal is requested before the programme start date; 50% if withdrawal is requested within 15 days after the start date; no refund after 15 days. Refunds are processed within 30 working days of approval.

## 3. Scholarships

### 3.1 Merit scholarship eligibility
Merit scholarships are awarded each term to students with a term CGPA of 3.5 or above (on a 4-point scale) and attendance of at least 80%. The merit scholarship waives 25% of the next term's tuition fee.

### 3.2 Need-based scholarship
Need-based scholarships require an application on the student portal with supporting documents within the first 3 weeks of a term. Applications are reviewed by the Scholarship Committee and the decision is communicated within 4 weeks of the application deadline.

### 3.3 Scholarship disbursement and continuation
Approved scholarships are adjusted directly against the next term's fee; no cash is paid out. A scholarship is withdrawn if the student receives a disciplinary penalty or their attendance falls below 75% in any term.

## 4. Course and specialisation changes

### 4.1 Elective course add/drop
Students may add or drop an elective course within the first 10 days of a term through the student portal without any penalty. After 10 days, a drop is recorded as "W" (withdrawn) on the transcript and needs approval from the Programme Chair.

### 4.2 Specialisation change (BDA to AI&DS or vice versa)
A change of specialisation between Big Data Analytics (BDA) and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI&DS) is allowed only once, at the end of Term 2 or Term 3. The student must submit a specialisation-change request form to the Programme Office at least 15 days before the next term starts, have a minimum CGPA of 3.0, and obtain approval from the Programme Chair. Credits from completed core courses are carried over.

## 5. Attendance and examinations

### 5.1 Attendance requirement
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for the end-term examination. Medical leave supported by a certificate submitted within 7 days is excluded from the attendance calculation.

### 5.2 Re-examination
A student who misses an end-term examination for a documented medical or family emergency may apply for a re-examination within 5 working days of the missed exam. A re-examination fee of INR 2,000 per course applies.

## 6. Campus services

### 6.1 Student ID card
Lost or damaged ID cards can be replaced by raising a support ticket with category "it_support". A replacement fee of INR 300 applies and the new card is issued within 3 working days.

### 6.2 Hostel
Hostel accommodation is allotted on a first-come, first-served basis at the start of the academic year. Hostel fees are billed separately from programme fees. Room change requests are considered only at the start of a term.
